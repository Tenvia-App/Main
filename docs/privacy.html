Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. This policy covers the Tenvia Android app and this website.

The short version

Tenvia does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you type into the app, and everything the app's AI writes back, stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The one exception is a chat you choose to send yourself (see below).

What the app does on your phone

  • Guides and AI chat. The safety guides and the AI model are stored inside the app. Your chat messages are processed by the AI on your phone and are never sent anywhere by the app.
  • Sending a chat on purpose (testers). Test builds have a button, "Send this chat to help improve Tenvia". It shows you the chat as text, lets you edit it (for example to remove names or places), and then opens your phone's normal share menu. The app itself sends nothing: the chat goes only where you send it, such as an email you write. Chats sent to the Tenvia team are used only to test and improve Tenvia's AI, are kept private, and have personal details removed before use. Shared chats go to tenvia.testchats@gmail.com; write to that address to have a chat you sent deleted.
  • Internet permission (test builds). Test builds ask for internet permission only so the app can talk to the AI running on the same phone (the address 127.0.0.1, which never leaves the device), and to check for a newer test version: once each time the app starts, if the phone is online, it reads Tenvia's public list of releases on GitHub. That request contains nothing you typed and no account or device ID; like any website visit, GitHub can see the phone's IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). If a newer version exists, the app shows a note with a Download button; it never downloads or installs anything by itself. The app makes no other connections. The finished app is planned to drop this permission.
  • Notifications and starting at boot. The app may show a notification while it gets the AI ready, and it can start that preparation when the phone turns on, so the AI answers faster in an emergency. No data is sent anywhere as part of this.
  • Chat history. Conversations are kept only while the app is open on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything it stored.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not contact 911 or anyone else for you. If you are in danger, call 911 yourself (in the US, call or text 988 for a mental-health crisis).
  • It does not use cookies, advertising IDs or third-party SDKs that collect data.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Tenvia adds no analytics or cookies. The preparedness checklist on the home page saves your ticks only in your own browser. GitHub, as the host, may log basic technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Tenvia collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked by opening an issue on GitHub.

Tenvia is test software and not for real emergencies. Read the disclaimer.
