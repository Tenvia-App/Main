# Disclaimer: test software, not for real emergencies

**Tenvia is an experimental prototype. Do not rely on it in a real emergency.**

- **If you are in danger, call 911** (or your local emergency number). In the US, call or text **988** for a mental-health crisis.
- Tenvia is **not** a medical device, a rescue service, or a replacement for training, a map, a first-aid kit, or professional help.
- Its advice has **not** been reviewed by medical, rescue or legal experts.
- The on-phone AI **can be wrong**. In blind tests it still gave unsafe or unhelpful replies to some messages (see [ai-experiments/RESULTS.md](ai-experiments/RESULTS.md)). Every test build shows "AI TEST BUILD: NOT FOR REAL EMERGENCIES" on the chat screen.
- Guide text is quoted from official government sources (US, Australia, New Zealand, UK, EU and Canada), which can be out of date or may not fit your situation.
- Test builds (APK files) are for testing only. Install them at your own risk.
