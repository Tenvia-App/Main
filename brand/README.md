# Tenvia emblem

**Final emblem:** [tenvia-icon.png](tenvia-icon.png). Adopted 7 November 2026 by Aries Fulkerson.

A small walking figure travels along an even-width S-shaped path between two gold hill shapes, each with a pine tree, on a dark green rounded square. It shows the meaning of the name, "hold on, there's a way through": someone making their way through.

Colors: dark green `#17231E`, amber `#D4A14A`, off-white `#EEE9DC`.

## Design history

ChatGPT produced the drafts. Aries Fulkerson directed every step: choosing concepts, spotting problems, testing with people and making the final decision. The files in [history/](history/) show each round.

| # | File | What it showed | Decision |
|---|------|---------------|----------|
| 1 | `01-six-concepts.png` | Six first concepts: Throughline, Ember, Cradle, Coil, Way In, Fold | Picked Throughline, because it shows the meaning (a way through) and reads well small |
| 2 | `02-throughline-centered-dot-eye.png` | An S-path with a dot in the middle | Rejected: the almond-shaped gap and centered dot read as a watching eye |
| 3 | `03-throughline-offset-dot-angry-eye.png` | The dot moved off-center | Rejected: a test viewer saw an "angry eye". The cause was the almond-shaped gap itself |
| 4 | `04-even-path-round-outline.png` | An even-width path, with the shapes joined into a circle | Rejected: the circle with a slash looks like a "no" sign or a coffee bean |
| 5 | `05-rolling-trail-broken-outline.png` | Separate offset shapes, the path running out to the edges | Twin Slopes was best; the dot still felt abstract |
| 6 | `06-walking-figure-final.png` | A walking figure on the path | Passed the "what do you see?" test |
| 7 | `07-walking-figure-with-trees-final.png` | Pine trees added to both hills, placing the path in the wilderness | **Final emblem**, adopted 7 November 2026 |
