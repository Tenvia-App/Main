# Testing Tenvia (test version 0.2.2)

Thanks for helping test **Tenvia**, a free offline app meant to help people through dangerous situations when there's no signal.

> **WARNING: This is a prototype. Do not rely on it in a real emergency.** In a real emergency, call or text 911 (or your local emergency number). The guides come from US government safety pages but haven't been reviewed by experts yet.

## Install (Android 8 or newer)

1. Get the file **`Tenvia-0.2.2-test.apk`** from Aries.
2. Tap it on your phone. Android will warn that the app is from an unknown source. That's normal for test apps that aren't on the Play Store yet.
3. If asked, allow your Files app or browser to **"Install unknown apps"**, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Open **Tenvia** from your app list (it's the gold path-and-walker icon).

Tenvia needs **no internet and no permissions**. You can check: Settings → Apps → Tenvia → Permissions shows none.

## What to try (10 minutes)

1. **Turn on airplane mode** first. Everything should still work.
2. In the big box, type what's happening **in your own words**, as if you were really stuck. For example:
   - "lost on a trail and it's getting dark"
   - "my car is stuck in snow and it's freezing"
   - "phone at 5% no signal"
   - something messy with typos or slang
3. Tap **Find help** and check whether the suggested guide fits.
4. Open a guide and step through it with **Next** and **Back**. Each step shows its official source.
5. Try a message about an injury. Tenvia should say it can't guide medical problems yet and point you to emergency services.

## What to tell Aries

- Did it pick the right guide? If not, **what did you type** and what did it pick?
- Was anything confusing, hard to read, or too small?

- What situation did you wish it covered?

Screenshots help a lot.
